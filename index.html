Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What does PPT stand for?

PPT is commonly used to mean PowerPoint presentation or a PowerPoint file. PowerPoint is presentation software; “PPT KANKER WALA” is our presentation-design service name.

Are the displayed prices final?

No. ₹149, ₹299 and ₹499 are starting prices. We confirm the final quote after reviewing the topic, slide count, complexity and deadline.

Does UPI payment get verified automatically?

No. The UPI link opens a compatible payment app. A submitted UTR is not proof of successful payment by itself; payment must be checked separately.

Can I choose pay after completion?

You can request this option, but the payment terms must be confirmed with us first. It is not physical courier cash-on-delivery.

How do I send images or source files?

After submitting the request, use WhatsApp to attach your reference files. This form does not upload files directly.
